# LSS flag-off SOLO census — results

Generated by `flag-off-lss-solo.sh`; protocol in
`flag-off-lss-loop.md`. Source of truth is
`benchmarks/flag-off-lss-solo.tsv`.

Each row is ONE run of the fixed shipping-compiler instrument
(`bin/eco-opt-solo-census`) self-compiling with exactly ONE flag off,
under `ECO_DISPATCH_STATS=1 ECO_MONO_LSS_REPORT=1`.

**Groups 2 and 3 are the flag's EFFECT** (what the analysis concluded).
**Groups 1 and 4 are COST** (what computing it took, on the shipping compiler).
What a flag BUYS at run time is out of scope — see the protocol's
"What this census does NOT measure".
